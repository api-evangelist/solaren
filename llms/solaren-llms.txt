# Solaren

> Solaren is a combination energy and aerospace company headquartered in Manhattan Beach, California,
> founded in 2001. It designs, develops, integrates, deploys and operates space solar power (SSP)
> plants — solar arrays on a patented lightweight satellite in geosynchronous orbit that convert
> sunlight to electricity, convert it to radio-frequency power, and beam it to an earth receiving
> station for delivery to the grid as baseload power. Solaren owns and operates its plants and sells
> electricity to utility and government customers.

## API surface

Solaren publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no SDK, and no machine-readable API
contract**. Software is not the product; the product is electricity generated in orbit and sold
under power purchase agreements. As of 2026-08-28 the following were probed on www.solarenspace.com
and none returned a machine-readable contract:

- /openapi.json, /openapi.yaml, /swagger.json, /v1/openapi.json, /api-docs, /docs, /redoc — HTTP 500 (WordPress error page)
- /graphql — HTTP 500
- /apis.json, /llms.txt — HTTP 500
- /.well-known/security.txt, /.well-known/openid-configuration, /.well-known/oauth-authorization-server,
  /.well-known/api-catalog, /.well-known/ai-plugin.json, /.well-known/agent-card.json,
  /.well-known/agent.json — HTTP 500
- No hosted MCP server, no A2A agent card, no gRPC/Protobuf, no WSDL, no AsyncAPI, no published SDK
  packages on npm/PyPI/Maven/NuGet/RubyGems/crates.io, and no company GitHub organization were found.

The only machine-readable endpoint served on the domain is the **default WordPress core REST API**
at https://www.solarenspace.com/wp-json/ (namespaces: wp/v2, oembed/1.0, wp-site-health/v1,
wp-block-editor/v1, wp-abilities/v1, plus the wpvulnerability plugin). It is content-management
infrastructure that ships with the CMS, not a Solaren product API, and it declares no company
namespace. It is recorded here for completeness and deliberately not catalogued as a Solaren API.
